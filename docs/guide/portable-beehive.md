# Portable Beehive

The Portable Beehive is a wearable chestplate that lets you deploy bees anywhere without placing a Mechanical Beehive block. It's worn in the chestplate slot or the Curios **back** slot.

## Features

- Carries up to **2 bee items** (Mechanical Bee or Bumble Bee)
- **Honey fuel system** — bees consume honey to operate
- **Upgrade grid** — install upgrades to enhance your swarm
- Forms a personal bee network centered on the player

## Fueling

The portable beehive runs on honey. You can fuel it by:

- **Shift+Right-click** with a honey bottle, honeycomb, or honey block in your off-hand
- **Create Spout** — place the beehive on a belt under a spout filled with honey fluid (250mB = 1 bottle of fuel)

The fuel bar is visible on the item's durability display.

## Upgrades

Open the beehive GUI to access the **upgrade grid**. Upgrades are placed on a grid and can be rotated to fit. Available upgrades include:

- **Rapid Wings** — increases bee flight speed
- **Swarm Intelligence** — allows more concurrent bees
- **Honey Efficiency** — reduces fuel consumption and break time
- **Honey Tank** — increases fuel capacity
- **Drone View** — enables the drone camera
- **Inventory Access** — lets bees access items in shulker boxes within connected inventories

See [Upgrades](/features/upgrades) for details on each.
